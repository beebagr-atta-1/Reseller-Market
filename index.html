How to install on your phone

Takes less than a minute. Follow these simple steps.

    Download the APK

    Tap Download APK above. If your browser warns you about the file, tap Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Open the file

    Open the notification when the download finishes, or find ResellerMarket.apk in your Files → Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Allow installation

    If asked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser or Files app, then go back.

  4. 4

    Install & open

    Tap Install, wait a few seconds, then tap Open. Sign up and start reselling!

Questions

Is it safe to install the APK?

Yes. This is the official Reseller Market app, downloaded directly from our website. Android shows a warning for every app installed outside the Play Store — this is normal.

Does it work on iPhone?

No, APK files only work on Android phones and tablets.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest APK from this page and install it over the existing app. Your account and data stay the same.

It says "App not installed". What do I do?

Make sure you have enough free storage, then uninstall any older copy of the app and try installing again.
